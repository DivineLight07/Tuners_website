About Us

At Tuners Club – Misr International University, we believe music is more than sound; it’s a language that unites people, sparks creativity, and builds community. Founded by passionate students, Tuners Club is MIU’s home for musicians, singers, and music lovers who want to share their talents, discover new rhythms, and create unforgettable experiences on campus.

🎶 Our Mission

We aim to inspire and empower students through music. Whether you’re a seasoned performer or just beginning your journey, Tuners Club provides a supportive space to explore your passion, collaborate with peers, and bring harmony to student life.

🎤 What We Do

Music Room: A fully equipped space on campus where members can practice, experiment, and enjoy playing together.

Courses & Workshops: Structured sessions designed to teach instruments, vocals, and music theory, guided by experienced members and instructors.

Jamming Sessions: Relaxed gatherings where students come together to play, improvise, and enjoy music collectively.

Community Building: Creating a welcoming environment where students connect through their shared love of music.

🌟 Our Vision

Tuners Club strives to be more than just a student organization. We envision a vibrant community where music is celebrated as a universal language, enriching campus life and leaving a lasting impact on everyone who joins.
