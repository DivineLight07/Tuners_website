About Our Club

Tuners MIU is the official music club at MIU, bringing together musicians and music lovers from all backgrounds and skill levels.

Our mission is to create a vibrant community where everyone can learn, collaborate, perform, and grow as artists. Whether you've been playing for years or just picked up an instrument yesterday, Tuners MIU welcomes you.
